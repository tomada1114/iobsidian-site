AgentCore Reader

This reader gives you a quick look at the whole topic before you start the learning roadmap. The roadmap teaches you how to build AI agents for companies on AWS. This reader has no hands-on steps. Each chapter takes about 12 to 15 minutes to read. The whole reader takes about two and a half hours. Read it in order, and the map grows step by step: first how agents work, then the AgentCore parts, then how to design them for a company.

11 chapters, about 2.5 hoursMatches roadmap stages S1 to S5

Analogy for this whole book

This reader compares an AI agent to hiring a new employee who thinks fast. The new employee's brain is the LLM, and Strands Agents plans the steps of the work. When you hire someone, you also need other things: a private room, memory, a toolbox, an ID badge, company rules, and a daily report. These are the AgentCore parts.

How to read this

  • If you are short on time, read only Chapters 1 and 2. You will still understand the whole roadmap.
  • At the end of each chapter, "Where you meet this on the roadmap" shows the row ID (the number in the course catalog) of the course where you use this content.
  • Each chapter's "Where companies get stuck" can also help you when you write Deep Dive notes in S3.

This reader is based on knowledge up to May 2026 and research notes from 2026-09-17. AgentCore adds features almost every month, so check the details in each course and in the developer guide.
