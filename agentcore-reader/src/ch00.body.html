AgentCore Reader

Thirteen short conversations about building AI agents for companies on AWS. After them you can explain what makes something an agent, and what each part of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore does. You can also say how to run an agent safely at a company.

13 pagesAbout 1 hour 45 minEnglish, B2 · dialogue

Who's talking

Maya is a staff engineer and the tech lead of Quickturn's platform. She helped her former employer put its first internal agents into production. Leo has just joined the team that owns Quickturn's tutor agent, and is following a study roadmap with stages S1 to S5. Each page is one of their one-on-ones.

Quickturn is a small company that makes a language-learning app, with a conversational tutor agent running on AgentCore. Maya, Leo and Quickturn are fictional.

How the pages fit together

The pages

  1. When is it really an agent? — One call, a workflow or a loop; choose the least autonomy that works.
  2. What changes when an agent goes to work at a company? — MCP, A2A, and the wall between a laptop and a company.
  3. What does AgentCore actually give you? — Separate parts around your code, added in order.
  4. Why let the model run the loop? — Strands is model-driven, and its harness layers give you control.
  5. Where does an agent actually run? — Runtime gives each session its own private room.
  6. How can an agent remember anything? — Short-term turns, long-term facts, and retrieval, always per user.
  7. How does an agent get the right tools? — One gateway, a few clear tools, checks both ways.
  8. Whose permission is the agent using? — Check who comes in, and choose whose rights go out.
  9. Why isn't 'please don't' enough? — Prompts ask; Policy enforces before a tool runs.
  10. How do you know an agent is doing a good job? — Trace every run, score quality, and loop.
  11. What worries a company when every team builds agents? — Central rules, shared cost, and three risks that must not meet.
  12. How do you design an agent you can defend? — Permissions in search, care with money, and "no agent" as an answer.
  13. What's left before the certification? — What AIP-C01 adds, and how to work through the last stage.

How to read this

The pages build a map in order, in about 8 minutes each. Maya is on the left, Leo on the right. Each page ends with the roadmap items where you meet its topic.
