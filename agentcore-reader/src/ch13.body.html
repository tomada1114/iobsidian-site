01The exam

AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional, exam code AIP-C01, assesses production use of foundation models and generative AI on AWS. AgentCore knowledge helps with agent applications, but the certification covers a wider set of implementation and operating decisions.

Official information checked on October 3, 2026 lists 75 questions, 180 minutes and a 300 USD price before local pricing details. Japanese is an available exam language. The guide identifies 65 scored questions, 10 unscored questions and a minimum passing scaled score of 750.

AWS describes a candidate with at least two years of production application experience on AWS or with open-source technologies. It also describes one year of hands-on generative AI implementation. Use that profile to judge preparation needs; it does not require another certification.

Read the current official guide before scheduling. Product coverage and exam details can change. This reader is a foundation for the agent-related work, and the guide is the authority for the exam's current scope.

02Mapping what you know

The reader's topics connect to several exam areas. Model choice and Strands relate to integration and orchestration. Runtime, Memory and Gateway relate to running agents and connecting tools. Identity and Policy contribute to security and authorization.

Observability and evaluations help with monitoring, testing and troubleshooting. Governance, caching and cost allocation support operational optimization. These connections provide a study map, but they are not an exact reproduction of the guide's five content domains.

Create a checklist using the official domain headings. Beside each objective, write a concrete task you have completed and can explain. A topic with no example beside it is a useful signal that reading has not yet become practical understanding.

03What the exam adds

Several areas need study beyond an introduction to agents. Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, supplies relevant source material to a model. Understand how documents are prepared, split into chunks, converted into embeddings and retrieved. An embedding is a numerical representation used to find related content.

Compare vector-store options such as OpenSearch Serverless, Aurora with pgvector and S3 Vectors in terms of the workload's retrieval and operating requirements. Also examine Bedrock inference options: on-demand calls, provisioned throughput, cross-region routing and batch processing. Their availability and billing depend on the model and region.

Prompt management covers storing, versioning and comparing prompts. Model evaluation compares candidate models or configurations against a defined standard. Agent evaluation additionally considers the behavior of a system that selects tools and performs several steps.

Review model customization and relevant SageMaker services where the guide includes them. Managed customization differs from developing or training a model from scratch. The guide places model development and training outside the target role’s scope. Use its objectives to determine the study depth.

04Working through S5

Begin the final study stage, S5, by reading the guide and marking objectives supported by completed hands-on work. Use a course or documentation to fill the unmarked areas, then take a practice assessment to expose misunderstandings.

For each wrong answer, identify the requirement or tradeoff being tested. Connect it to a system you have built or inspected. Without such an example, perform a small task demonstrating the concept. Then explain why the other choices would not satisfy the requirement.

An application project often needs depth in a few services; an exam requires breadth across its scope. Plan time for services and options that your current tutor project does not use. A practice score helps direct that work, but the goal is being able to explain the design decision behind each answer.

The complete design connects the parts. Entry authentication verifies callers; Identity supplies workload identities and credentials. Runtime hosts execution, and Strands coordinates model and tool calls. Memory retrieves authorized context; Gateway and Policy control covered tools. Observability and evaluation provide evidence about behavior.

A certification can communicate knowledge, and a concrete design explanation shows how that knowledge is applied. Keep examples of the tradeoffs you tested: permission boundaries, failure handling, cost and quality. They are useful for both the exam and a real design review.
