A busy morning

It was eight in the morning, and the coffee shop was full. Ben stood in a long line. He was tired, and he wanted his coffee fast.

At the counter, he asked for a hot black coffee. The young man at the counter wrote "Ben" on a paper cup. Then Ben went to the side to wait.

Many people were waiting there too. The workers called out names, one after another. "Ana! Mike! Sam!" It was very loud.

Not his drink

"Ben!" a worker called. Ben took the cup with his name on it. He walked to a small table by the window and sat down.

He took a drink. Then he stopped. The coffee was sweet and cold, with lots of milk and ice. It was an iced latte, a cold coffee drink with milk.

Ben looked at the cup again. It said "Ben" in big letters. So it was his name, but it was not his coffee. He did not know what to do.

The other cup

Then he heard a voice at the next table. "Oh no," a woman said. She was looking at her cup with a strange face.

Ben looked over. Her cup was hot, and he could see the black coffee inside. On the side, it said "Ben" too.

"Excuse me," Ben said. "I think you have my coffee. And I think I have yours." He turned his cup so she could see the name.

The woman laughed. "My name is Jen," she said. "I guess Ben and Jen sound the same in here."

A good swap

They made a swap. Ben gave her the iced latte, and she gave him the black coffee. "Sorry, I already took one drink," Ben said. "Me too," Jen said, and they both laughed.

Jen asked if he came here often. Ben said yes, every morning before work. She said she was new in the area. So Ben told her about a quiet park near the shop.

They talked until their cups were empty. The next morning, Ben saw Jen in the line again. This time, she waved and said, "Black coffee, right?"
