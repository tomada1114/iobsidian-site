A red light

Nina got to the office early on her first day. She wore her best shirt. In her hand was a new plastic badge with her photo on it.

She held the badge up to the small black box next to the door. The light turned red. She tried again. Red again.

Through the glass, she could see people at their desks. She could knock. But she did not want her first moment at the new job to be a problem. So she just stood there and waited.

A man with two coffees

After a few minutes, a tall man came up the stairs. He had a coffee in each hand and a bag on his shoulder.

"Are you trying to get in?" he asked.

"Yes," Nina said. "I'm new here. Today is my first day. My badge doesn't work."

The man smiled. "That happens to everyone. It usually starts working in the afternoon." He turned so his bag was near the box. "My badge is in the front pocket. Could you get it for me?"

Nina laughed and took out his badge. The light turned green, and the door opened.

Coffee for the new person

Inside, the man put one coffee on the table. "I'm Leo. I sit over there, by the window." Then he gave her the second cup. "I got two by mistake. Do you want this one?"

Nina did not really believe it was a mistake. But she said thank you, and she took it. The coffee was warm, and she felt better.

One month later

A month later, Nina came back from lunch. A young man stood by the door with his badge in his hand. The light was red.

"First day?" she asked.

He looked a little scared. "Yes. Could you let me in?"

"Of course," Nina said, and she opened the door. "It starts working in the afternoon. Do you drink coffee?"
