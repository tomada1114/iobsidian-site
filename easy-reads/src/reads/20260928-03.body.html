A lid that will not open

Walk down a street in a small Colorado mountain town. You may see big metal trash cans with heavy lids. To open one, you push a small handle or turn a hook with your fingers.

This is not to stop people. It is to stop bears. These cans are called bear-proof trash cans. A person can open them in a second. A bear, with its big paws, cannot.

Hungry in the fall

The Rocky Mountains are home to many black bears. In the fall, these bears get very hungry. They need to get fat before winter, when they sleep for months and do not eat.

So in the fall, a bear eats for most of the day. It looks for berries, nuts and anything else with a lot of energy. And a bear has a very good nose. It can smell food from far away.

Trash is easy food. It does not run away, and it is full of fat and sugar. A trash can on a street is like a free meal for a bear.

A bear that learns

Bears are smart, and they remember. If a bear finds food in a town once, it comes back. Soon it may walk into yards, cars and even houses.

A bear that is not afraid of people can hurt someone. When that happens, the bear is often moved far away or even killed. People in these towns say, "A fed bear is a dead bear."

So the best way to help a bear is to never let it find trash in the first place.

Living next to bears

Many mountain towns have rules about trash. People keep their cans inside a garage until the morning the truck comes. Some towns ask people not to hang bird food in the fall.

Visitors need to be careful too. At a campground, you often put food in a metal box, not in your tent or car. It is a small thing to do. It keeps both people and bears safe.

Words to notice

lid
the top part that covers a box or can
bear-proof
made so that a bear cannot open it
paws
the feet of an animal like a dog or bear
energy
what the body gets from food to move and live
campground
a place where people stay in tents outside

Think about it

What wild animals live near where you live? Do people have any rules because of them?
