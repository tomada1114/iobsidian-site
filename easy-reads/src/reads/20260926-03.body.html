An early start

It is a cool Saturday morning in the fall. The college football game starts at three in the afternoon. But at nine in the morning, the parking lot is already filling up.

Cars and trucks come in one by one. Many people wear the colors of their team. Some bring chairs, tables and big bags of food. They are not late for the game. They are early for the party.

Opening the tailgate

A family parks their pickup truck, a truck with an open back. The father opens the tailgate, the door at the back of the truck. It folds down and makes a small flat table.

This is where the party gets its name. People call it tailgating. Fans with cars open the back door of the car in the same way. They put food and drinks there.

Next, the family sets up a small grill, a metal box for cooking food over fire. Soon they cook hot dogs and burgers. The smell moves across the lot. People from the next car come over to say hello.

Games in the lot

After lunch, two boys set up a game called cornhole. Each player throws small bags filled with corn at a wooden board. The board has a hole near the top.

A bag in the hole gets three points. A bag on the board gets one point. The game is easy to learn, so kids and grandparents play together. Other people throw a football back and forth.

Some people bring a small TV. They watch other games from around the country. Music plays from a car radio. It feels like a big picnic.

Time to go in

About an hour before the game, the family starts to clean up. They put out the fire in the grill. They pack the chairs and close the tailgate.

Then they walk with thousands of other fans to the stadium, a very big place for sports with many seats. For many fans, the day in the parking lot is as fun as the game itself.
