A busy evening

It was a busy Monday evening at the gym. Ken came in after work with a small bag. He put his phone, his keys and his shoes in a locker, a small metal box with a door and a lock. He closed the door and walked away fast.

He ran for thirty minutes. Then he lifted weights. The music was loud, and the room was full of people. Ken felt tired but happy.

Which one was it?

At nine forty, Ken went back to the locker room. He looked at the long rows of gray doors. They all looked the same. He did not know his number.

"Was it 112? Or 211?" he said to himself. He tried the lock on 112. It did not open. He tried 211. It did not open. His key was in the locker, and his phone was in the locker too.

A voice came from the speaker. "The gym closes in fifteen minutes." Closing time is the time when a place shuts its doors for the day. Ken started to worry.

Help at the front desk

Ken walked to the front desk, the place near the door where workers help members. A young woman was there. She was putting clean towels on a shelf. A towel is a soft cloth you use to dry your body.

"I forgot my locker number," Ken said. "Everything is inside."

The woman smiled. "That happens a lot," she said. She gave him a towel and walked with him. "What color is your bag?"

"Blue. With a white line."

She had a master key, one key that opens every lock. She opened the doors one by one and looked inside. Ken helped her. Some lockers were empty. Some had other people's bags.

Just in time

At the ninth door, Ken saw a blue bag with a white line. "That's it!" he said. It was locker 121.

"Thank you so much," Ken said. "You stayed late for me."

"No problem," she said. "Next time, take a photo of the number."

Ken laughed. He took his bag and walked out at ten, just in time. The next week, he took a photo of his locker number on the first day.
