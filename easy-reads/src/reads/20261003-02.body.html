A call at ten

Mia is a web engineer. Last week she sent her resume to a software company. Two days later, she got an email. It asked for a thirty-minute call with a recruiter. A recruiter is the person who finds and talks to new people for a company.

Mia thought it would be a hard test. She looked at her old code all night. But this first call is not about code. People call it the phone screen. It is a short first check, before any technical interview.

The first ten minutes

At ten o'clock, her phone rings. The recruiter says hello and tells her about the team. Then he asks, "Can you tell me about yourself?" Mia talks for about two minutes. She talks about her last job and one project she liked.

Next he asks, "Why do you want to work here?" He wants to see real interest. Mia says she uses the company's app every day. He smiles and writes a note.

The basic questions

Now the recruiter asks short, simple questions. When can she start? Is she talking to other companies? Can she work in the office three days a week? Does she need help to work in this country?

Then comes the question many people fear. "What pay range are you looking for?" A pay range is a low number and a high number. Mia gives a range she found before the call. The recruiter says it fits the job.

None of this is about skill. The recruiter checks interest, timing, location and pay. If one of these does not match, a long interview helps no one.

Why it is short

The recruiter talks to many people each day. Engineers on the team are busy, and their time costs a lot. So the company asks the easy questions first. This saves time for everyone, Mia too.

At the end, he asks, "Do you have any questions for me?" Mia asks about the next steps. He says a technical interview comes next. The call ends after twenty-eight minutes. Mia closes her laptop. She did not write one line of code.

Words to notice

recruiter
a person who finds and talks to new people for a company
phone screen
a short first call before the real interviews
technical interview
an interview that checks your skills, like coding
pay range
a low number and a high number for pay
next steps
what happens after this part is done

If a recruiter asked you "Why do you want to work here?", what would you say?
