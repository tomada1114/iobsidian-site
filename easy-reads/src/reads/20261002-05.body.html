A new face at the table

On Tuesday after class, Mina walked into the city library. She was new at the school. Her teacher had put her in a study group for a class project.

Three students sat at a big table near the window. Their names were Jake, Rosa and Leo. They smiled and said hello. Then they started to talk about the project.

The project was a poster about the history of their town. It was due on Friday. The group had a lot of work to do.

Too fast to follow

Jake talked fast. Rosa talked even faster. Leo laughed at everything and added more ideas. Mina tried to follow, but the words went by like cars on a busy road.

She wanted to say something. Each time she opened her mouth, someone else started to speak. So she listened and wrote notes in her small notebook.

After an hour, the group had a problem. They had too many photos and too many facts. The poster was a mess, a thing with no clear order. Nobody knew where to start.

"We need more time," Rosa said. "But we don't have it," Jake said. They all looked tired.

One small idea

Mina looked at her notes. She saw that every fact had a year. She took a deep breath.

"Maybe," she said in a soft voice, "we can put everything on a timeline." A timeline is a line that shows events in order of time. "Old things on the left, new things on the right."

The table was quiet for a moment. Then Leo said, "That's simple. I like it." Rosa took a long piece of paper and drew a line across it.

A warm ending

Now the work was easy. Each person took a few photos and put them in the right place on the line. Mina read the years from her notebook. In thirty minutes, the poster looked clear and nice.

When they packed their bags, Jake turned to Mina. "Next time, talk more," he said with a smile. "We need your ideas." Mina smiled back. She felt like part of the group.

On Friday, the teacher looked at the poster for a long time. "This is very easy to follow," she said. Rosa pointed at Mina. "That was her idea."
