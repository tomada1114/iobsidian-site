Getting ready

Mia had three friends coming to stay for four days. She lived in a small house with one spare room, a room that is free for guests. She cleaned it all morning. She put fresh sheets on the bed.

The spare room had one bed for two people. For the third friend, Mia had an air mattress. An air mattress is a bed you fill with air. You can put it away when you do not need it.

At four in the afternoon, she went to get the pillows. She opened the closet. There was only one extra pillow.

The friends arrive

At five, the doorbell rang. Sam, Lena and Jo stood outside with their bags. They were tired from the long drive, but they were smiling.

"Come in," Mia said. "Make yourself at home." This means "relax and act like this is your own house." Sam put his bag down and took off his shoes. Lena went to the kitchen for a glass of water.

Mia showed them the spare room. Then she told them the problem. "I have one extra pillow. I am sorry. I did not check."

Solving it together

Jo laughed. "That is easy. I always sleep with a small pillow. I can use my sweater." She took a soft sweater from her bag and folded it into a square.

Lena had another idea. "Your couch has big cushions. Can we use one?" Mia said yes. A couch cushion is not a real pillow, but it is soft and flat. Lena said it was fine for her.

Sam took the real pillow. "I will blow up the air mattress," he said. "That is my job." It took him ten minutes. At the end, his face was red, and everyone laughed.

A good first night

They ate dinner together and talked until late. Then everyone went to bed. The sweater, the cushion and the pillow all worked.

In the morning, Jo said she slept very well. Mia made coffee for everyone. After breakfast, the four friends went to the store. They bought two new pillows for the spare room.

Words to notice

spare room
a free room in a home, often for guests
air mattress
a bed you fill with air
make yourself at home
relax and act like it is your own house
cushion
a soft thing you sit or lean on
blow up
to fill something with air

Think about it

Have you ever stayed at a friend's home? What did your friend do to help you feel at home?
