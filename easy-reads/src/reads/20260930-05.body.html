A picture on the floor

Ken had a new picture for his living room. It was a photo of the sea, in a nice wood frame. He wanted to put it on the wall above the sofa.

He looked in his tool box. He had a hammer. He had some nails. But the picture was heavy, and the wall was not wood. A nail would not hold it. He needed something else.

He knew what it looked like. He knew the word in his own language. But he could not find the English word. So he put on his shoes and walked to the hardware store, a shop that sells tools and parts for the home.

No word, only hands

A young clerk stood near the door. "Hi. Can I help you find something?" she asked.

"Yes, please," Ken said. Then he stopped. The word was still not there. He felt his face get warm.

He held up one finger. "It is small. It is made of plastic. It looks like a little tube." He made a small shape with his hands.

The clerk looked at his hands. "Okay. What do you do with it?"

What does it do?

This question helped a lot. Ken did not need the name now. He only needed to say what the thing did.

"It is the thing you use to hold a screw in the wall," he said. "First you make a hole. Then you push this thing into the hole. Then you put the screw in. It gets bigger inside, so the screw stays."

The clerk smiled. "Oh, you mean a wall anchor. Follow me."

She took him to a long shelf. There were many small bags of wall anchors. "How heavy is the picture?" she asked. Ken showed her with his arms. She gave him a bag of strong ones.

A new word to keep

At home, Ken made two holes in the wall. He pushed in the anchors and put in the screws. The picture went up, and it did not move.

Later, he told a friend about it. "I forgot the word," he said. "But I told her what it does. That was enough." Now he knows the word. He also knows another way to ask for something.

Words to notice

hardware store
a shop that sells tools and parts for the home
clerk
a person who helps people in a store
tube
a long, thin shape that is empty inside
screw
a small metal pin you turn to push into things
wall anchor
a small part that holds a screw in a wall

Think about it

Pick one thing in your kitchen. How would you ask for it without its name? Say what you use it to do.
