A trip to the farm

In October, a family drives out of town to visit a pumpkin patch. A pumpkin patch is a farm field where pumpkins grow. The leaves are red and yellow, and the air feels cool. Near the gate, a small shop sells warm apple cider, a sweet drink made from apples.

Not every farm offers the same activities. Some have only pumpkins and a quiet walk. Others have music, food, games, or a hay ride. A family can check the farm’s website before leaving to learn about the activities, prices, and opening times.

Choose an activity

A hay ride is a slow trip in an open cart pulled by a tractor. Blocks of hay, or dry grass used to feed animals, make seats. The cart may go past fields, trees, or cows. The ride can be bumpy, so a quiet walk may be a better choice for someone who does not like bumps.

Some farms also make a maze from tall corn plants. Paths turn left and right, and some end at a dead end. A map can help, but finding a way out is part of the fun for many visitors. It takes time, so a family can decide whether everyone wants to walk.

Find a pumpkin

In the field, pumpkins sit on the ground in rows. Some are round; others are tall or bumpy. Visitors can choose from many sizes. One may look just right but feel too heavy to carry. Another may have a soft spot. At last, each person finds one to take to the shop.

Before the trip, it helps to think about how the pumpkin will be used. A small one is easy to carry. A larger one may be better for carving. To carve a pumpkin is to cut shapes into it. People may cut a face, put a light inside, and place it outside for Halloween at the end of October.

Make the day fit

The farm may be sunny at noon and cold later. Comfortable shoes are useful for walking on grass or dirt. If the weather changes, visitors can take a break or skip an activity. There is no need to do everything.

A good visit can be a long day with rides and games, or a short walk to choose one pumpkin. The family can look at its time, energy, and budget, then pick the parts that sound enjoyable.

Words to notice

pumpkin patch
a farm field where pumpkins grow
hay
dry grass used to feed farm animals
maze
a set of paths with choices and dead ends
carve
cut shapes into something
choose from
pick one or more things in a group
at last
after waiting or trying for some time

English to notice

A pumpkin patch is a farm field where pumpkins grow.

The where-clause adds information about a place. It tells what happens there.
