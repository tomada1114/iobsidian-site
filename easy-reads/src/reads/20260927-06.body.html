A painting in Paris

The Mona Lisa is a small painting of a woman with a quiet smile. Leonardo da Vinci painted it about five hundred years ago. For a long time, it has hung in the Louvre, a large art museum in Paris.

Before 1911, many art lovers knew the painting. But it was one good painting among many. Most people in the world did not know its name.

An empty wall

One day in August 1911, the painting was gone. Someone took it off the wall and walked out with it. At first, the museum workers did not worry. They thought someone was taking a photo of it.

Then they understood. The Mona Lisa was stolen. The museum closed for a week. The police looked everywhere, but they could not find it.

Many people came to the museum just to look at the empty wall. They wanted to see the place where the painting once was.

Two years of news

Newspapers wrote about the theft, the act of stealing, again and again. They printed the woman's face on the front page. People in many countries saw it for the first time. They read the story and talked about it.

Months passed, and there was no news of the painting. Some people thought it was lost forever. But the story did not go away. Each new story made the face more famous.

Found in Italy

In 1913, the painting came back. The thief was an Italian man who had worked at the Louvre. He kept the painting in his room in Paris for two years. Then he took it to Florence, a city in Italy, and tried to sell it.

The man who met him checked the painting and called the police. The thief was caught. The painting went back to Paris, and big crowds came to see it.

So the Mona Lisa was already a fine painting before 1911. But the theft and the news gave it a new kind of fame. Today, it may be the best known painting in the world.
