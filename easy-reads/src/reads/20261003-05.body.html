Something in the dirt

On Saturday morning, Ken went for a short hike. The trail went up a small mountain. It was cool and quiet, and the trees smelled fresh.

About halfway up, he saw something shiny in the dirt. He picked it up. It was a car key with a black button and a small red ribbon on it.

Ken stopped and looked around. Nobody was there. "Someone will need this," he said to himself. "They can't drive home without it."

What should he do?

Ken thought about his choices. He could leave the key on a rock. But the owner might walk down a different path. He could take it to the police. But then the owner would wait in the parking lot for hours.

Then he had a simple idea. Every hiker comes back to the trailhead, the place where the trail starts. The owner would come back there too.

He walked back down. At the trailhead, there was a wooden board with maps and notices. Ken found a pen and an old receipt in his bag.

A note on the board

He wrote a short note: "Did you lose a car key with a red ribbon? I found it on the trail. I am sitting at the picnic table by the parking lot."

He put the note on the board with a pin. Then he sat at the table, ate an apple and waited. Birds sang in the trees. It was not a bad way to spend an hour.

The owner comes back

After about forty minutes, a woman came down the trail. She went to her car and checked her pockets. Then she checked them again. Her face turned pale.

She looked at the board and read the note. She walked quickly to the picnic table. "Is it a key with a red ribbon?" she asked. Ken held it up.

"Thank you so much!" she said. "My daughter put that ribbon on it. She said it would help me find it." Ken laughed. "Well," he said, "it helped me find you."

Words to notice

trail
a small path through a forest or over a mountain
trailhead
the place where a trail starts
ribbon
a thin, long piece of soft cloth
receipt
a small paper you get when you buy something
pale
with very little color, often from fear

Think about it

Have you ever lost or found something important? What happened, and how did it end?
