A name that tricks you

In the United States, fall has a smell. It is warm and sweet. People call it pumpkin spice. You can find it in coffee, cookies, bread and even candles.

But here is the surprise. Most pumpkin spice has no pumpkin in it. The name does not mean "spice made from pumpkin." It means "the spice you put in pumpkin pie."

Pumpkin pie is a sweet pie that many American families eat in the fall. The pumpkin itself does not have a strong taste. The spices give the pie most of its flavor, the taste and smell of a food.

Four spices in one jar

You can buy pumpkin spice as a small jar of brown powder. Most jars have four main spices. The first is cinnamon, which comes from the bark of a tree. It is sweet and warm.

The second is ginger, a root with a sharp, hot taste. The third is nutmeg, the seed of a tree. It has a soft, deep taste. The last is cloves, small dried flower buds. Cloves are very strong, so a jar has only a little.

These spices came to America from far away. For a long time, people used them in fall and winter baking. So the smell already felt like cold days and holidays.

How a coffee made a season

In the early 2000s, a big coffee chain made a new drink. It was a latte, coffee with a lot of hot milk. They added pumpkin pie spice and sugar. At first, the drink had no real pumpkin in it at all.

The drink sold only in the fall. That was the smart part. People could not have it all year, so they waited for it. When it came back each year, it felt like a sign that fall had started.

Other companies saw this and made their own pumpkin spice things. Soon there were pumpkin spice cereals, snacks and ice cream. Some people love it. Some people make jokes about it. But almost everyone knows the smell.

Make it at home

You do not need to buy a jar. Mix a lot of cinnamon with some ginger and nutmeg. Then add just a little cloves. Put a small spoon of it in coffee or on toast, and you have the taste of an American fall.
