A strange week begins

Maya is sixteen. She goes to a high school in a small American town. This week is not a normal week. It is Homecoming week.

On Monday, Maya wears her pajamas to school. So do her friends. Even some teachers come in soft pants and slippers. Nobody is in trouble. It is Pajama Day.

Every day this week has a theme, a special idea for the day. People call them spirit days. On a spirit day, students dress in a fun way to show school spirit, a feeling of pride in their school.

Spirit days all week

On Tuesday, Maya and her friends dress like people from an old movie. On Wednesday, everyone wears a funny hat. On Thursday, each grade wears its own color.

On Friday, the whole school wears the school colors. Maya's school colors are blue and white. The halls look like a sea of blue. In the afternoon, there is a pep rally, a big meeting in the gym with music and cheers. The band plays, and everyone shouts.

The game and the dance

On Friday night, Maya goes to the football game. Her school plays at home. The stands are full of students and families. Many older people are there too.

Maya's dad points to a man near them. "He played on this team thirty years ago," he says. Many former students, people who went to the school in the past, come back for this game.

Many schools also have a Homecoming dance, often on Saturday night. Maya puts on a nice dress. She goes with a group of friends. They dance, take photos and laugh until late.

Where the name comes from

On Sunday, Maya asks her dad a question. "Why do we call it Homecoming?"

"It started as a day to welcome former students back to their school," he says. "They came home. That is still the idea." Colleges in the US have Homecoming too, and many graduates come back for it.

Maya thinks about the man at the game. Someday, she will come back too.
