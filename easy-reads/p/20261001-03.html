Why Elk Sing in the Mountains Each Fall

Every fall, a strange high call fills the mountains of Colorado. It comes from a very large animal. Here is why it calls.

Nature366 wordsAbout 4 min

A sound in the trees

In the fall, the mountains of Colorado get cold at night. Just as the sun goes down, you may hear a long, high sound. It starts low, goes up very high, and then ends with a few short grunts.

The sound does not come from a bird. It comes from a male elk, a very large deer. A big male can be taller than a person. His call is called a bugle, because it sounds a little like a horn.

Why the males call

Fall is the time when elk find mates. This time is called the rut. Male elk try to keep a group of females close to them. The bugle helps with this.

The call says two things. To the females, it says, "I am here, and I am strong." To other males, it says, "Stay away. These are mine." A loud, long call shows that the male is big and healthy.

Sometimes another male calls back. Then the two may walk toward each other. They may push and fight with their antlers, the large bones that grow on their heads. Most of the time, one male just gives up and walks away.

Big body, high voice

A large animal usually has a deep voice. So why is the elk call so high? Scientists found that elk can make two sounds at the same time. One sound is low. The other is very high and goes over it.

The high part travels far through the cold air. It can move across a wide open field and into the trees. That helps the call reach other elk who are far away.

Going out to listen

Many people drive to Rocky Mountain National Park in the fall just to hear the elk. They come at dusk, the time just after the sun goes down. The elk walk out of the forest into open grass, and the calls begin.

People stay in their cars or stand far back. The males are not calm at this time of year, and they can be dangerous. Park workers ask visitors to keep a safe space and to stay quiet. In the dark, people just listen.

What sound tells you a new season has started where you live? When do you hear it?
